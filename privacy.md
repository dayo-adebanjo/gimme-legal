# Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

gimme is run by its developer ("we," "us"). This policy explains what the gimme app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

**The short version:** we collect only what gimme needs to work. We don't sell your data, we don't run ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. gimme has no analytics.

## 1. What we collect

### Account

- Your email address, from Apple or Google when you sign in. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email," we get a private relay address instead.
- Your first name from Apple or Google, which we use as your display name. You can change it in the me tab.
- Your sign-in method and a unique account ID.

### What you save

- Links you save or share into gimme, plus the item name, image, price, currency, store name, and category we read from the page or that you enter.
- A copy of each item's image, stored by us so it doesn't disappear when the store changes its page.
- Photos you choose from your photo library to replace an item's image.
- Which items you've planned or bought, when, and the price you paid.

### Your budget and settings

- Budget type (weekly or monthly), amounts, home currency, which budget period each item is planned into, and any leftover budget you choose to roll over.
- Your time zone and the first day of your week, taken from your phone.

### Your device

- Our servers see the IP address of each request, which we use for security and to limit abuse.

### Store pages your phone loads for us

Some stores block our servers from reading their product pages. When that happens, your phone may load the page itself and send us the page's contents so we can read the product details. This is a plain request from the app. It doesn't use your Safari or store logins.

### Things we don't collect

- No analytics or advertising identifiers, and no tracking across other companies' apps or sites.
- No contacts, precise location, or health data.
- We read your clipboard only when you tap Paste.
- gimme cannot see anything else in the apps you share from. It receives only the link you send.

## 2. How we use it

- To run gimme: sign you in, save and show your items, keep your budget and plan, and sync between your devices.
- To read product details from the links you save.
- To sort items into categories automatically.
- To convert prices between currencies using daily exchange rates.
- To keep gimme secure, stop abuse, and fix problems.
- To answer you when you contact support.

We don't use your data to build advertising profiles.

## 3. Who else handles your data

We use these service providers. They process data only to provide their service to us.

| Provider | What it does for gimme | What it receives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supabase | Database, sign-in, image storage, and the servers that run gimme | Everything in section 1 |
| Apple and Google | Sign in with Apple and Google | Your sign-in request; they tell us your ID, email, and first name |
| Anthropic | Picks an item's category | The item's name, store, link, and page description. Not your email or account details |

Other things to know:

- **Stores.** When you tap Buy, you leave gimme and open the store's site or app. The store follows its own privacy policy.
- **Exchange rates.** We fetch daily rates from public sources. No personal data is sent.
- **Legal and safety.** We may disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect people from harm, fraud, or abuse.
- **If gimme changes hands.** If gimme is sold or transferred, your data may move with it, under this policy or one at least as protective.

We don't sell your personal information and we don't share it for advertising.

## 4. How long we keep it

- Your account data stays until you delete your account.
- When you delete an item, it's removed after a few seconds (the time you have to undo), including its stored image.
- When you delete your account, we remove your account, items, budgets, and stored images.

## 5. Your choices and rights

In the me tab you can:

- **Edit** your items, budget, display name, and home currency.
- **Delete** an item, or your whole account and everything in it (Your data, then Delete account).
- **Sign out** at any time.

For a copy of your data, or anything else, email [gimme.wish@gmail.com](mailto:gimme.wish@gmail.com). We'll reply within 30 days. If you live in a place with privacy laws that give you additional rights, such as California, you can use those rights the same way. We won't treat you differently for using them.

## 6. Security

Each account's data is separated from every other account's at the database level. Data is encrypted in transit and stored with our providers, images are kept private, and no secret keys are kept in the app. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't promise absolute security. If a breach affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

## 7. Children

gimme is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email [gimme.wish@gmail.com](mailto:gimme.wish@gmail.com) and we'll delete it.

## 8. Where your data is handled

gimme is run from the United States and is offered here. Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. For significant changes, such as collecting new kinds of data, we'll tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
