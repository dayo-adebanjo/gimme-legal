# gimme-legal

Home page, Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for the gimme iPhone app, published with GitHub Pages at
https://gimme-wish.com/privacy/ and https://gimme-wish.com/terms/.

Edit `privacy.md` or `terms.md` and push to `main`; GitHub rebuilds the site in about a minute.
Update the "Effective" date when the text changes.

`index.html` is the home page: a full-screen background from `assets/` (`mobile-bg.png` in
portrait, `web-bg.png` otherwise; both carry the logo and tagline), the phone screenshot in the
middle, and buttons to the two legal pages. The legal pages use `_layouts/default.html`.
